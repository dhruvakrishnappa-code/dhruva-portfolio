03. Projects

featured project

Phishing URL Detector - India Edition

A web app that estimates the phishing risk of a suspicious link without ever visiting it. Paste a URL and get a 0-100 risk score, a LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH verdict, the reasons behind it, and a downloadable PDF report. Tuned for Indian scam themes: UPI fraud, fake bank KYC, Aadhaar and PAN phishing.

  • Random Forest model (scikit-learn) plus rule checks over 30+ URL features
  • 79.4% recorded accuracy on real test data, 43 automated tests
  • Flask + SQLite backend, scan history dashboard, PDF reports
  • Deployed on both Vercel and PythonAnywhere
